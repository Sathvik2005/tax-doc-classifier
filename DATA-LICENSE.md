# Data

`data/criteria.json` and the evaluation manifest in `eval/` are derived from
forms published by the United States Internal Revenue Service. Works of the
United States Government are not subject to copyright (17 U.S.C. § 105). The
derived files are released under the same Apache-2.0 licence as the code.

The evaluation downloads IRS PDFs directly from irs.gov at run time; no PDF is
redistributed here.

The Jev backend calls the TypeSafe API under TypeSafe's own terms of service.
Nothing in this repository grants access to that API.
